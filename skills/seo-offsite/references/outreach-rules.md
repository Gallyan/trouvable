# Outreach rules

## Before writing

- The venue's rules are read and dated. "Not read" means no draft.
- There is a reason this recipient would care, specific to their page (an item missing from their
  list, an outdated fact, a gap their readers ask about). No reason, no email.
- The user has said which address and name they write from.

## The message

- Says who is writing and their relationship to the product, in the first two lines.
- Names the exact page and what is proposed (add, correct, update), with the facts needed.
- Short: under ~150 words (heuristic, design choice of this skill, to calibrate).
- No invented praise ("I loved your article") unless the user says it is true.
- No link request phrased as a condition, no payment, no gift in exchange for a link or review.
- No reciprocal-link offer.
- Gives an easy way to say no, and does not ask twice in the same message.

## Follow-ups

- One follow-up after 7–10 days, then stop (heuristic, design choice of this skill, to calibrate).
- A reply of "no" or a request to stop ends the thread; set the row to `declined`.

## AI-written text

- If the venue forbids AI-written submissions or posts: give the user its rules and a bullet list
  of facts only. Write no prose for it.
- If the venue requires disclosure, the draft includes it.
- Drafts are drafts: the user rewrites in their own voice before sending.

## Communities

- Contribute before promoting; read the self-promotion rule and any ratio it states.
- Never suggest asking friends to upvote, posting from several accounts, or coordinating votes.
- One venue, one post: no cross-posting of the same text unless the venue allows it.

## Email and SMS rules in France (CNIL)

Read on 2026-10-25: cnil.fr/fr/communication-electronique-quelles-regles (page dated 10 June
2026) and article L34-5 of the Code des postes et des communications électroniques (Légifrance).

- L34-5 defines prospecting as any message « destiné à promouvoir, directement ou
  indirectement, des biens, des services ou l'image d'une personne ».
- To consumers (B2C), prospecting « repose, en principe, sur le recueil préalable du
  consentement », except existing customers for « produits ou services analogues fournis par la
  même personne ».
- To professionals (B2B), « Le consentement préalable n'est pas systématiquement requis » when
  the message is « en lien avec l'activité professionnelle de la personne contactée »; the
  recipient is told and can object simply, in every message.
- A message with « une part de contenu commercial » can be « requalifié en prospection
  commerciale ».

For outreach to editors and site owners: write to a professional address about their own page,
say who you are, give a one-line way to opt out, and honour it (row set to `declined`).
