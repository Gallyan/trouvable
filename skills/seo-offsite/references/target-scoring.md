# Target scoring

Four criteria, each scored 0–3. Scale, criteria and thresholds are a **heuristic, design choice
of this skill, to calibrate**. A red flag (see `red-flags.md`) excludes a target whatever its score.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fit** — does the venue's audience match the site's buyers? | unrelated | adjacent | same field | exact niche and market |
| **Editorial legitimacy** — is there a human choice, real content, visible moderation? | auto-accepts anything | thin, mostly listings | edited, some rejected entries | curated, clear criteria, maintained |
| **Signs of life** — recent activity readable for free | nothing in 12 months | sporadic | regular | active and referenced elsewhere (e.g. cited by assistants, linked from peers) |
| **Effort to fit** — hours and rule fit, inverted | rules exclude it / >4 h | 2–4 h or conditions hard to meet | 1–2 h | <1 h, rules clearly allow it |

Total /12. Suggested reading:

- 9–12: first 30 days.
- 6–8: days 31–90, if hours allow.
- ≤5: kept in the tracker as `parked`, not worked.

Each score carries one line of evidence (URL, literal rule or observation with date). Scores for
signs of life are `measured` from what is visible; never infer traffic or authority metrics.

## Sizing the first 30 days

- Weekly hours `H` (default 2, `assumed`). Plan at most ~75 % of `4 × H`; the rest absorbs
  rejections, rewrites and follow-ups.
- Effort per action (`estimated`, to calibrate): directory or list submission 0.5 h, community
  first contribution 1 h, personalised outreach email 0.5 h, guest piece or interview 3–6 h.
- Order: entity card and owned profiles first, then highest-score targets, one community at most
  in the first month (communities reward presence before promotion).

## Local business variant

Replace Fit with **Local relevance** (same service area). Add review work as its own row: ask real
customers, after the service, through the platform's normal flow. Check the review platform's
current policy (read and dated) on incentives and on filtering who is asked before suggesting any
wording.
