# Google Business Profile rules

Read on 2026-10-25 on Google's own pages. Re-open them at run time, compare, and date the
reading in the report; a row that no longer matches the page is dropped.

Source: "Guidelines for representing your business on Google",
support.google.com/business/answer/3038177 (French version `?hl=fr`; no update date shown).

| Point | What the page says | What to check on the profile |
|---|---|---|
| Eligibility | Practitioners "can be contacted directly at the verified location during stated hours"; FR: « directement joignable à l'adresse validée, pendant les horaires d'ouverture indiqués » | Someone really receives customers at that address during the stated hours |
| Service-area business | « vous devez masquer votre adresse professionnelle » when customers are not received there | Address hidden, service area set to the real towns served |
| Name | « Vous ne devez pas inclure d'informations superflues dans le nom de votre établissement » (examples given: location such as "near SOHO", taglines, store codes) | Name identical to the shop sign, documents and site; no town, service or slogan added |
| Address | « Les boîtes postales ou boîtes aux lettres situées à des adresses distantes ne sont pas autorisées »; a virtual office "isn't eligible for a Business Profile" | A real, staffed place; no domiciliation or coworking mailbox |
| One profile | « Il ne doit y avoir qu'une seule fiche par établissement »; "A practitioner shouldn't have multiple Business Profiles to cover all of their specializations" | Duplicates found by searching the name and the phone in Maps |

Use Google's own wording in reports ("superfluous information in the name"), not agency
jargon. A rule the page no longer states is not quoted.

## What a profile check covers without owner access

From the public profile in Google Maps (date and place of the search noted): name, category
shown, address or service area, phone, site link, hours, photos, number of reviews, date of the
latest review, replies from the owner. Anything else (secondary categories, attributes,
verification state, performance figures) needs the owner's access: ask for screenshots or mark
it `not measured`.
