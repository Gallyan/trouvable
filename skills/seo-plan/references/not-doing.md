# Not-doing list

Pick the items relevant to this user (always include anything they asked about). Each item:
what, why not now (or never), legitimate alternative. Where an item breaks a search engine or
platform policy, read the current policy page and quote it with the date; do not quote from memory.

| Not doing | Why | Legitimate alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Buying links or link packages | Link-scheme policy risk; no control over where links land | seo-offsite: listings and outreach that say who you are |
| Mass-generating pages with AI or templates | Scaled-content policy risk; thin pages dilute the site | A few pages from seo-content-brief, each checked with seo-fact-check |
| Targeting the biggest head terms on a young site | Page one is held by much larger sites | seo-keyword-verdict on specific, winnable queries |
| Translating the site before the main language is indexed | Multiplies unindexed pages | seo-indexing first; seo-multilingual decides later |
| Adding schema to "get stars" | Markup does not guarantee rich results; must match visible content | seo-schema on facts actually on the page |
| Checking rankings daily | Noise; costs hours | Weekly routine and Search Console checkpoints |
| Redesign or migration "for SEO" without a cause | Risk of losing what works | seo-audit first; fix specific causes |
| Fake reviews, review gating, vote rings | Platform rules; trust | Ask real customers through the platform's normal flow |
| Hidden text or "notes for AI" | Cloaking / hidden-text policies | Clear on-page facts; geo-mentions to measure |
| More posts when existing ones are not indexed | Content is not the binding constraint | seo-indexing, seo-internal-links |
| Chasing every tool score (DA, "SEO score 100") | Third-party metrics, not results | Metrics sheet tied to the business model |

The "Why" column states the risk; it does not claim a specific penalty.
