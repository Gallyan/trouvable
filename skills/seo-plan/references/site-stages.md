# Site stages

Stage boundaries are a **heuristic, design choice of this skill, to calibrate**. Use the evidence
available; when two stages fit, site age breaks the tie and the stage is marked `provisional`.
Without Search Console data the stage is always `provisional`.

| Stage | Name | Typical evidence | What the plan focuses on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pre-launch | no live site, or a placeholder; not indexed | Structure, a few target topics, entity card, launch-ready technical basics. Cheap, reversible work |
| 2 | Young | live for less than ~6 months, or few indexed pages; almost no non-brand impressions | Pages reachable and indexable, internal links, 3–5 winnable queries, first listings |
| 3 | Emerging | indexed; non-brand impressions exist but clicks are low; a handful of pages bring most of them | Improve near-page-one pages, fill topic gaps around what works, first outreach |
| 4 | Growing | steady non-brand clicks across several sections | Scale what works per section, refresh decaying pages, comparison and authority content |
| 5 | Established | many sections bring traffic; team or process exists | Maintenance, freshness, governance, international or new sections; plan by workstream |

## Special cases (still give a stage)

- **Many pages, no traffic** ("200 posts and no traffic"): check indexing before content quality;
  often stage 2 behaviour on a stage 3–4 page count. The binding site constraint is usually
  indexing or demand fit, not volume.
- **Old domain, new site**: age of the current site counts, not the domain registration.
- **Migration in the last 3 months**: flag it; figures before and after are not comparable.
- **Local business**: stages apply, but listings and reviews arrive earlier in the plan.

## Writing the stage line

`Stage {n}, {name} ({provisional|confirmed}): {evidence, labelled}; {what broke the tie}`
